## Design principles

- Explicit contracts between systems
- Idempotent actions and retries
- Strong auditing and PII controls
- Human-in-the-loop for sensitive decisions

## Observability

Dashboards track run success rates, latency, and cost. Alerts integrate with PagerDuty or Slack.
