# Why now

Finance teams carry the burden of repetitive work—invoice processing, reconciliation, reporting. **AI agents** change the baseline by executing workflows with context, memory, and policies.

> AI agents don't just automate tasks—they understand context, make decisions, and adapt to your business processes in real-time.

## Core use cases

Finance operations span multiple complex workflows. Here are the primary areas where AI agents deliver immediate value:

- **AP automation and three-way match**: Automatically match purchase orders, invoices, and receipts with 99.9% accuracy
- **Journal entry creation and validation**: Generate and validate journal entries based on business rules and policies
- **Cash application and reconciliation**: Match payments to invoices and reconcile bank statements automatically
- **Close checklists and variance explanations**: Ensure all close tasks are completed and provide variance explanations

## Key Outcomes

Organizations implementing AI agents in finance see measurable improvements:

### Speed
- **70% faster cycle times** for monthly closes
- **50% reduction** in days sales outstanding
- **Instant** reconciliation instead of days

### Accuracy
- **99.9% accuracy** on structured data processing
- **Zero manual errors** in data entry
- **100% audit trail** for all transactions

### Visibility
- **Real-time status** for auditors and leadership
- **Automated reporting** with drill-down capabilities
- **Proactive alerts** for anomalies and exceptions
